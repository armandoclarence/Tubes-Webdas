Sacred Architecture

The Central Symmetrical Axis

The entire layout of the Forbidden City adheres strictly to the layout rules of the Central Symmetrical Axis. All principal palaces and ceremonial halls line up perfectly along a single straight path running from south to north, symbolizing the Emperor's position as the stable center of the cosmos.

Philosophy of Imperial Colors

The palette chosen for the palace carries profound symbolic weight. Walls are painted crimson red to signify joy, energy, and prosperity, while the roofs are covered in yellow glazed glass tiles—a color restricted exclusively to the imperial family to represent supreme power and divinity.

Mythical Roof Guardians

Perched along the ridges of the palace roofs are rows of carefully sculpted mythical animal guardians. Beyond serving as spiritual protectors to ward off evil and lightning fires, the total number of beasts indicates the strategic rank of the building itself. The most sacred hall features the maximum count of ten guardian figures.

The Core Dougong Joint Technique

Remarkably, these monumental wooden pavilions were built completely without using a single metallic nail. Master craftsmen utilized an ancient traditional interlocking bracket technique known as Dougong, whose natural flexibility allows the complex to safely absorb energy and survive hundreds of major earthquakes over the centuries.

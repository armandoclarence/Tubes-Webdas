Daily Life in the Court

Behind its imposing ceremonial facades, the Forbidden City sheltered an intricate, deeply insular daily court life. The Inner Palaces formed a private world for thousands of imperial consorts and concubines who lived in immense luxury, yet navigated subtle internal rivalries to secure favors from the line. Attending to them were the royal eunuchs—highly trusted servants who managed everything from internal household affairs to state administration secrets. Fascinatingly, the imperial families favored animal companions; they bred purebred Pekingese dogs given dedicated palace quarters, and took great care of hundreds of friendly palace cats—whose descendants still freely roam and protect the historic museum grounds to this day.

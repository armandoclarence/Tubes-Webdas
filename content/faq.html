Enjoy your vacation

For beginners, it is highly recommended to studythe Qin Dynasty (the first unification of China and the origin of the Great Wall) and The Tang Dynasty (the golden age of culture, poetry, and the Silk Road). If you are interested in magnificent palaces like the Forbidden City, consider learning about the Ming and Qing Dynasties.

The best time to visit is during autumn (September - November). The weather is very cool, the sky is clear, and the golden leaves complement the red walls and yellow roofs of the imperial palace beautifully.

You must try Peking Duck, which was once a exclusive dish for emperors, and Dim Sum, which has a close connection to the tea culture (Yum Cha) of ancient Silk Road merchants.

Xi'an is a top choice after Beijing. This city is the starting point of the Silk Road and the location where Terracotta Army (Terracotta Warriors) of Emperor Qin Shi Huang was buried.

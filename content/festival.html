Imperial Festivals

The seasonal rhythms within the Forbidden City revolved around sacred rituals and magnificent court celebrations. During the Imperial Lunar New Year, thousands of finely crafted gold silk lanterns illuminated the outer courts, and the Emperor personally brushed calligraphy characters to bestow good fortune upon the land. Grand Coronation ceremonies and Imperial Weddings featured spectacular processions with thousands of guards parading across the courtyard plazas. Each major celebration concluded with formal Royal Banquets, where exquisite dishes were presented on the finest imperial porcelain plates, accompanied by traditional court music and classical Chinese opera performances.
